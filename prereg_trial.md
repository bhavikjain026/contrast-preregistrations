# PRE-REGISTRATION — randomised pilot: targeted vs general practice after a diagnosed gap

2026-10-13. Approved by the team lead (with changes) and published as v7 in bhavikjain026/contrast-preregistrations
before the first participant. Choices marked **[CHOICE]** were proposed by the drafter and accepted by the team lead.

## 1. Status and ethics
- **The trial starts only after Plaksha University ethics approval.** No participant is approached before it. Approval
  reference and date are added as a dated addendum (published) before the first participant.
- No participant data exist for this study. The volunteer pilot (P01–P08) is a separate study with its own
  pre-registration.

## 2. Question and hypothesis
After a diagnosis from 16 answers and one shared lesson, does practice targeted at the learner's diagnosed gap reduce
error one week later more than general practice of the same length?
- **H1 (primary):** among learners with a diagnosed gap, delayed-test error, adjusted for pretest error, is lower with
  targeted than with general practice.
- This is a **pilot**. Power applies to the **gap stratum**, not to all 30–40 randomised: with the registered check (D1:
  80% power, two-sided α 0.05, ANCOVA with pre–post r 0.5) the minimum detectable d is 0.79 at 20 per arm and 0.92 at 15
  per arm; if 50–80% of 30–40 people have a gap (8–16 per arm), it is d ≈ 0.89–1.31 (same formula, `src/power_ancova.py`).
  The effect estimate and its CI are the main result; a non-significant result is not evidence of no effect.

## 3. Participants
- 30–40 consenting Plaksha undergraduates, **18 or older** (adults only). Written informed consent at visit 1.
- **[CHOICE] Not eligible:** members of the project team; anyone who took part in the volunteer pilot (P01–P08) or used
  the tutor before (prior exposure to the same items and lessons).
- **[CHOICE] Recruitment:** open call to all undergraduate years; first come, first served until 40 randomised or the
  enrolment window closes (end date fixed in the ethics application). No payment unless ethics requires one.

## 4. Design and procedure
Two-arm, parallel, individually randomised (1:1), two visits.
**Visit 1 (≈ 60 min, one sitting, on the frozen tutor build):**
1. **Pretest:** 16 fixed problems (same for everyone, same order), no feedback. **Design rule (fixed now; exact
   items in the dated addendum):** 8 one-number-changed pairs on the tutor's story grid — **3 base-rate pairs whose base
   rates are ≥ 60 points apart** (as the replication's best fixed quiz, base 10% → 70%; the two problems of a pair may
   come from two stories, each within its allowed ranges), 3 false-alarm pairs and 2 hit-rate pairs, each chosen by the
   largest change in the correct answer; never hit = 1 − false alarm; no problem repeated. Answers in whole percent;
   typed working optional (used only for the rater measure, §7).
2. **Diagnosis:** the tutor's learner profile fitted on the 16 pretest answers (intercept + base + hit + false-alarm
   weights; use = learner weight ÷ correct-learner weight; a word only if 2 SE ≤ 0.3). **Diagnosed gap** = the most
   under-used ingredient with a definite word ("ignores it", "under-uses it" or "uses it the wrong way"); otherwise
   **no gap** (all used fully, or "not sure yet"). Stratum = gap / no gap.
3. **Shared lesson:** the same count-table lesson for everyone (about 8 minutes, fixed text and examples).
4. **Randomisation** (§5), then **practice** — equal item count, time and feedback in both arms:
   - **Targeted:** 8 problems chosen so that ignoring the diagnosed ingredient changes the correct answer by ≥ 10
     points (one-number-changed pairs on that ingredient).
   - **General:** 8 problems covering the three ingredients equally (no relation to the diagnosis).
   - **No-gap learners in the targeted arm** get the general set (the routing policy has no target for them), so the two
     arms differ only in the gap stratum.
   - Both arms: same feedback after each item (correct answer + count table), no branch lesson text, 15-minute cap.
5. **Immediate post-test:** 12 fixed problems (form A), no feedback.
**Visit 2 (7 days later; window 6–9 days):** **delayed test**, 12 fixed problems (form B, parallel to A), no feedback.
- **[CHOICE] Test forms:** A and B each = two one-number-changed pairs per ingredient (base, false alarm, hit), new numbers
  and stories not used in the pretest or practice; generated by a fixed-seed script; the exact item lists and their
  SHA-256 are published as a dated addendum before the first participant.

## 5. Allocation and blinding
- **Allocation:** permuted blocks of 4 within each stratum (gap / no gap), from a seeded script (`seed` kept secret);
  the list's SHA-256 is published before the first participant and the list is revealed after the analysis. **[CHOICE]**
- **Concealment:** the person running sessions does not hold the list; after the pretest and diagnosis, the tutor
  requests the next allocation for that stratum from the list held by a second team member (or a sealed allocation file
  the session software reads only at that moment). Allocation cannot be changed after it is revealed.
- **Blinding:** participants are told only that they will "practise"; the session runner can see the practice content
  (not blinded). **Outcomes are scored automatically** from the numeric answers by the frozen scoring script, so no
  human marker judges correctness; the analysis file keeps arm labels separate until the analysis is run. The rater
  measure (§7) uses blind coders who see no arm, diagnosis or answers.

## 6. Primary outcome and analysis
- **Primary outcome:** mean absolute error (percentage points) on the 12 delayed-test problems.
- **Analysis:** ANCOVA, delayed error ~ arm + pretest error (mean absolute error on the 16 pretest problems), two-sided
  α 0.05; report the arm effect (targeted − general) with 95% CI, p and d (effect / pooled SD).
- **Everyone is randomised** (stratified by gap / no gap). **Population (primary): the gap stratum** — participants with a
  diagnosed gap and a delayed test, analysed in the arm allocated — the only group in which the two arms receive
  different practice. **The number with a gap (and by ingredient) is reported.**
- **Expected dilution:** in the whole-policy analysis (all randomised, secondary 1) no-gap learners get the same practice
  in both arms, so its effect is expected to be about (share with a gap) × (gap-stratum effect), with less precision per
  person; it answers a different question (does the routing policy help a whole class?).
- Script: `src/trial_analysis.py` (SHA-256 09ef600e3864d17ae64ab676cb94b8f5aeca3a78a2d1d089f5eab7331fc4b3c3, commit
  3d82be9), tested end to end on simulated data (`tests/test_trial_analysis.py`, 7: recovers a known gap-stratum effect,
  the whole-policy effect is diluted by the gap share as expected, matches the closed-form ANCOVA, ≈ 95% CI coverage
  under no effect, missing-data paths, never overwrites). Any change before unblinding is published as a dated addendum.

## 7. Secondary outcomes (reported whatever they show; no correction for multiple tests)
1. **Whole routing policy (intention to treat):** the same ANCOVA on all randomised participants with a delayed test.
2. **Base-rate-gap learners only** (diagnosed gap = base rate): the same ANCOVA — the base-rate label is the one that
   replicated (split-half κ 0.56–0.73 across three datasets), whereas false-alarm and hit labels are fragile (κ ≈ 0.3).
3. Immediate post-test error (same ANCOVA, gap stratum).
4. **Gap-item error:** among gap learners, delayed-test error on the problems that involve their diagnosed ingredient.
5. Whole-policy ANCOVA with the stratum added as a covariate.
6. **Neglecters caught / lessons wasted** (as in the pilot): two blind raters code the pretest typed working (B/F/H/A/U,
   visit label = one code on ≥ 6 of the pretest items with working — **[CHOICE]** adapted from 6 of 8); codable = both
   raters agree and not U. Caught = rater B and the profile's diagnosed gap is the base rate, out of all rater-B
   participants; wasted = profile says base rate, raters say A, F or H.

## 8. Missing data, deviations, exclusions
- **Missing delayed test:** primary = available cases (gap stratum). Sensitivity (gap stratum): (i) immediate
  post-test carried forward, (ii) arm-mean imputation. Numbers missing per arm reported.
- **Out-of-window delayed tests** (outside 6–9 days): included in the primary; sensitivity excludes them.
- **Exclusions:** none for performance. Before randomisation: people who do not finish the pretest are not randomised
  (listed). After randomisation: only withdrawal of consent (their data are deleted; counted). Protocol deviations
  (e.g., session interrupted, wrong practice set shown) are listed and the participant stays in the allocated arm.
- Help (calculators allowed; no other help): **[CHOICE]** asked at the end of each visit; reported, not an exclusion.

## 9. Tutor version
The tutor is frozen as a git tag **before the first participant** — the build that includes the "replication lessons"
changes (to be specified, approved and built separately; not part of this draft). The tag name and commit are published
as a dated addendum before enrolment. No tutor change after that tag until the last delayed test.

## 10. Data handling and reporting
Session logs and answers are kept off git (git-ignored), pseudonymous codes only; only derived numbers are reported.
All registered outcomes are reported, including null or adverse results, with deviations listed.
