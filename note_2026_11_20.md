## 1. Report v24 (tag v24-report): what "derived" covers

The report says every number comes from its output "except where marked 'derived', which means subtraction of two printed counts". Two places do not fit that definition:

- The shares 50%, 30% and 33% in the Q1x line are divisions, not subtractions: 9 of 18, 6 of 20 and 5 of 15. They are marked "derived" but are not subtractions.
- The line "Leaving out 100% − false alarm cost ChatGPT one Q1 set, Gemini one and Claude none" is a subtraction of printed counts (18 − 17, 20 − 19, 16 − 16) but is not marked "derived".

Read "derived" as "arithmetic on printed counts", and read the second line as derived.
