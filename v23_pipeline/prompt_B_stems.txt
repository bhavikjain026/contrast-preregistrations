CONVERSION RULES (from plan section 5; the last two bullets state how we read cases the plan leaves open, fixed before generation)
- A percentage is taken as written: 2.5% gives 2.5.
- A bare decimal in [0, 1] is a probability, multiplied by 100, unless the text says percent: 0.025 gives 2.5, and 1 gives 100.
- "1 in N" and "k of N" (also "k out of N") are stored as exact fractions of 100: 1 in 2,500,000 gives 100/2500000, and 8 of 10 gives 800/10. Do not simplify or compute decimals.
- With counts (natural frequencies), each rate is a "k of N": b = ill of total, h = positive ill of ill, f = positive healthy of healthy. - Specificity or a true-negative rate of s gives f = 100 − s. A miss or false-negative rate of m gives h = 100 − m. A false-positive rate is f as stated. A detection rate or sensitivity is h as stated. Write 100 − s exactly, for example 100 − 95.5 = 4.5.
- An overall accuracy without conditional rates gives excluded:overall_accuracy. A test called "95% accurate" with no other rates counts as an overall accuracy.
- Nothing is rounded.
- Our reading (fixed before generation): a bare number greater than 1 with no % sign, in a rate's place, is taken as a percentage.
- Our reading (fixed before generation): standard English number words are converted ("one in a thousand" gives 100/1000). Anything you are unsure how to convert gives excluded:unconvertible.
